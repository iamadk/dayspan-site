Dayspan Privacy Policy

Updated November 15, 2026 for Dayspan 1.4.

Your cards and storybooks

Dayspan stores your dates, notes, photos and storybooks on your device. The widget reads the selected card or book and its thumbnail locally. When you choose a picture, the app receives the images you select using the system photo picker. Dayspan does not require an account.

Sharing a memory

You decide what to share. A chat invitation contains the moment title and your question. You can paste the reply into your storybook. A PDF or editable ZIP includes the moments you choose, including saved perspectives and photos. Someone you send a file to can keep their own copy. Gift drafts stay on your device until you choose to export. Purchases are processed by Apple.

Optional usage statistics

If you choose Allow statistics, Dayspan sends limited usage events to TelemetryDeck. These include event time, card count ranges, widget setup, return visits, which upgrade action was used, and actions such as creating, styling, editing, previewing, importing and exporting stories or sharing a question. An app-specific random identifier is hashed on your device so repeat use can be counted. Reports exclude titles, questions, answers, photos, filenames, chat apps and recipients. Dayspan has no advertising or cross-app tracking.

You can turn off Help improve Dayspan in Settings at any time. Pending delivery is cancelled and the local queue and identifier are removed. Enabling statistics again creates a new identifier. Reports already delivered cannot be recalled by switching off. For information about the service, visit TelemetryDeck.

For questions about your data or deletion, contact iamadk@gmail.com. Reports use a random identifier and may not be assignable to an individual.
