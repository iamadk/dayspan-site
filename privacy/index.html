Dayspan Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Dayspan does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party.

  • No accounts. Dayspan has no sign-up, login, or user profiles.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app contains no analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or trackers of any kind.
  • Your data stays with you. Everything you enter (dates, titles, photos) is stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, data is stored in your private iCloud database, which only you can access — we cannot read it.
  • Photos. When you choose a photo as a card background, Dayspan uses Apple's out-of-process photo picker and can only access the specific photos you select. Photos never leave your device or your private iCloud.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment information.

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page before it takes effect.
