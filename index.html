A storybook for the dates you share

One date.
Two memories.

Count the days since you met. Ask what they remember, put both answers on one page, and send them a storybook PDF to keep.

Your first two-perspective page is free.
Storybooks require Dayspan 1.4 or later.

How it works

  1. Start with a date.

    Pick a date card or add a moment to your storybook. A photo and a few words can take you right back.

  2. Ask for their side.

    Send a question in your usual chat. They reply there, and you paste their words beside yours.

  3. Give them the page.

    Export a PDF and send it to someone you love. They can read it on their phone without installing Dayspan.
